# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Master Services Agreement** (the "**Agreement**") is entered into between:

**Meridian Analytics GmbH**, a company registered in Ireland (company no. IE-552010), registered office 2 Grand Canal Square, Dublin 2 ("**Meridian**" or the "**Supplier**"); and

**the Customer** identified in the Order Form.

This Agreement is provided on Meridian's standard terms. **Annual Charges:** £75,000 per annum.

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Customer Data**" means data, including personal data, submitted by the Customer to the Services.

1.2 "**Services**" means the analytics services described in the Order Form.

---

## 2. Term and Termination

2.1 The initial term is twelve (12) months from the Effective Date.

2.2 The Agreement automatically renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice.

---

## 3. Charges and Payment

3.1 The Customer shall pay all invoices within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date.

3.2 The Supplier may increase the Annual Charges at any time during the term, in its discretion and without a stated cap, on thirty (30) days' notice to the Customer.

---

## 4. Limitation of Liability

4.1 Each party's aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement shall be limited to one-and-one-half (1.5) times the Annual Charges.

4.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or loss-of-profit damages.

---

## 5. Indemnification

5.1 The Customer shall indemnify the Supplier against third-party claims arising from the Customer's use of the Services, including intellectual-property and data-breach claims and other third-party claims, provided that the Customer's total indemnity liability shall not exceed the limit of liability in clause 4.1.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Each party retains its pre-existing and background intellectual property. The Supplier grants the Customer a licence to use the Deliverables for the term of the Agreement.

---

## 7. Data Protection

7.1 The processing of personal data is governed by the Supplier's Data Processing Agreement (with EU Standard Contractual Clauses), which the parties agree is UK GDPR-compliant.

7.2 The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any personal-data breach without undue delay and within seventy-two (72) hours.

7.3 The Supplier shall give prior notice of new sub-processors and flow down equivalent data-protection obligations.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, for five (5) years following termination.

---

## 9. Governing Law

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ireland, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Dublin.

---

## 10. Assignment

10.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent; either party may assign to an affiliate on notice.

**Signed** for and on behalf of the parties as of the Effective Date.
