# SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

**This SaaS Subscription Agreement** (the "**Agreement**") is made on our Standard SaaS Subscription Terms (approved template, version-locked in the CLM) between:

**Northwind Cloud Ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales (company no. 09112345), registered office 14 King's Road, Reading RG1 3AA ("**Northwind**" or the "**Provider**"); and

**the Customer** identified in the Order Form.

**Annual Subscription Fee:** £18,000 per annum.

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Customer Data**" means data, including personal data, submitted by the Customer to the Service.

1.2 "**Service**" means the Northwind subscription software described in the Order Form, which includes AI-assisted features.

---

## 2. Term and Termination

2.1 The initial term is twelve (12) months from the Effective Date.

2.2 The Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.

---

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 The Customer shall pay the Annual Subscription Fee within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

3.2 The Provider may increase fees on renewal by no more than the greater of CPI or five percent (5%), on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

---

## 4. Limitation of Liability

4.1 Each party's aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement shall be limited to the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

4.2 Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, consequential, or loss-of-profit damages.

---

## 5. Indemnification

5.1 The Provider shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims that the Service infringes intellectual property rights, and against losses arising from a breach of the Provider's data-security obligations. Each party's indemnity obligations are capped at the limit of liability in clause 4.1.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Each party retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing and background intellectual property. The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use the Deliverables for the term of the Agreement.

---

## 7. Data Protection

7.1 The parties shall comply with the Data Processing Agreement (incorporating the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and EU Standard Contractual Clauses) attached as Schedule 1, which governs the processing of personal data in the Customer Data.

7.2 The Provider shall notify the Customer without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of any personal-data breach.

7.3 The Provider shall maintain an up-to-date list of sub-processors, give the Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior notice of any intended changes, and flow down equivalent data-protection obligations to each sub-processor.

7.4 On termination, the Provider shall return or securely delete all Customer Data within thirty (30) days at the Customer's election.

---

## 8. AI Features

8.1 The Provider shall not use Customer Data to train its models without the Customer's prior written opt-in.

8.2 The Customer acknowledges that AI-generated output is provided for assistance only, does not constitute legal advice, may contain errors, and should be reviewed by a qualified person before reliance.

---

## 9. Acceptable Use and Availability

9.1 The Customer shall not use the Service for any unlawful purpose; the Provider may suspend access for material breach on notice.

9.2 The Provider commits to 99.9% monthly uptime, with service credits as the Customer's remedy as set out in the Order Form.

---

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, for three (3) years following termination.

---

## 11. Governing Law

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

---

## 12. Assignment

12.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld; either party may assign to an affiliate on notice.

**Signed** for and on behalf of the parties as of the Effective Date.
