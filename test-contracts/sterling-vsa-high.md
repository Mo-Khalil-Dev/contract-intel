# VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Vendor Services Agreement** (the "**Agreement**") is entered into between:

**Sterling Vendor Services Pte Ltd**, a company registered in Singapore (UEN 201744210K), registered office 1 Raffles Place, Singapore 048616 ("**Sterling**" or the "**Vendor**"); and

**the Customer** identified in the Order Form.

This Agreement is provided on Sterling's standard terms. **Annual Charges:** £140,000 per annum.

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Customer Data**" means data, including personal data, submitted by the Customer to the Services.

1.2 "**Services**" means the managed services described in the Order Form.

---

## 2. Term and Termination

2.1 The initial term is twelve (12) months from the Effective Date.

2.2 The Agreement renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives sixty (60) days' notice of non-renewal.

2.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience only on not less than one hundred and twenty (120) days' prior written notice.

---

## 3. Charges and Payment

3.1 The Customer shall pay all invoices within forty-five (45) days of the invoice date.

3.2 The Vendor may increase the Annual Charges on renewal by no more than five percent (5%) on sixty (60) days' notice.

---

## 4. Limitation of Liability

4.1 Each party's aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement shall be limited to three (3) times the Annual Charges.

4.2 This Agreement does not exclude or limit either party's liability for indirect, consequential, or loss-of-profit damages, which shall be recoverable in full subject only to clause 4.1.

---

## 5. Indemnification

5.1 The Customer shall indemnify the Vendor against any and all third-party claims arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or the Customer's use of the Services, including but not limited to intellectual-property claims, data-breach claims, and general commercial third-party claims, up to an aggregate cap equal to the limit of liability in clause 4.1.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. The Customer grants the Vendor a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide licence to use any materials, feedback, and configurations the Customer provides in connection with the Services.

---

## 7. Data Protection

7.1 The processing of personal data is governed by the Data Processing Agreement attached as Schedule 1, incorporating Standard Contractual Clauses.

7.2 The Vendor shall notify the Customer of any personal-data breach within seven (7) days of becoming aware of it.

7.3 The Vendor may appoint sub-processors at its discretion without prior notice to, or consent from, the Customer, and is not required to flow down its data-protection obligations to such sub-processors.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, for three (3) years following termination.

---

## 9. Governing Law

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Singapore.

---

## 10. Assignment

10.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent; either party may assign to an affiliate on notice.

**Signed** for and on behalf of the parties as of the Effective Date.
