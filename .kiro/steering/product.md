# Product Overview

## What is ContractIntel?

ContractIntel is an AI-powered contract analysis and due diligence platform designed for legal operations teams. It automates contract review by scanning documents against organizational playbooks, identifying risk flags, and streamlining the approval workflow.

## Core Value Proposition

- **Automated Analysis**: Upload contracts (PDF/DOCX) and receive AI-powered risk analysis in ~60 seconds
- **Playbook Compliance**: Automatically compare contracts against internal standards and acceptable ranges
- **Risk Flagging**: Identify critical, medium, and low-risk clauses with recommendations
- **Workflow Management**: Track contracts from upload → AI review → reviewer sign-off → approval
- **Renewal Tracking**: Monitor upcoming renewals with urgency indicators
- **Audit Trail**: Complete history of all contract actions and decisions

## Target Users

- **Legal Operations Managers**: Portfolio oversight, metrics, prioritization
- **Reviewers / Senior Counsel**: Flag investigation, risk assessment, sign-off
- **Head of Legal**: Final approval, compliance oversight
- **Business Owners**: Contract status visibility

## Key Workflows

1. **Upload & Analyze**: Drop contract → AI scans against playbook → flags generated
2. **Review & Annotate**: Reviewer investigates flags → adds notes → resolves/dismisses
3. **Approve & File**: Head of Legal approves → contract filed to register → renewals tracked
4. **Playbook Management**: Define standard positions and acceptable ranges per clause type
5. **Renewal Management**: Track upcoming renewals with notice periods and urgency alerts

## Phase 1 Scope (Current)

- Home dashboard with portfolio metrics
- Contract upload and ingestion
- AI-powered clause extraction (Claude API)
- OCR processing (Google Document AI)
- Risk scoring and flagging
- Contract detail view with flags
- Playbook comparison
- Renewals tracking
- Team management
- Audit log

## Design System

- **Brand**: Professional, trustworthy, efficient
- **Colors**: Blue primary (#2563EB), semantic risk colors (red/orange/green), neutral palette
- **Typography**: DM Sans (UI), DM Mono (data/numbers)
- **Components**: 13 reusable components (buttons, badges, cards, modals, navigation)
- **Screens**: 8 core screens (Home, Portfolio, Upload, Processing, Results, DeepDive, Compare, Renewals, Settings, Export)

## Success Metrics

- Time to review: <5 minutes per contract (vs. 30+ minutes manual)
- Risk detection: 95%+ accuracy on critical clauses
- User adoption: 80%+ of legal team using platform within 3 months
- Renewal tracking: Zero missed renewal deadlines
